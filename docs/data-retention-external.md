# Data retention and disposal policy

**Service:** [SERVICE NAME]
**Published by:** [ORGANIZATION]
**Effective:** [DATE] · **Version:** 1.0 · **Review cycle:** annual

This policy states what personal information [SERVICE NAME] holds, how long it is kept, how
it is destroyed, and how you exercise your rights over it. It is written to meet the
retention and disposal obligations of Canada's *Personal Information Protection and
Electronic Documents Act* (PIPEDA), Quebec's *Law 25*, and the *EU/UK General Data
Protection Regulation* where those apply to you.

The governing principle is PIPEDA's Principle 4.5, which the GDPR shares in its storage
limitation principle: **personal information is retained only as long as it is needed for
the purpose it was collected, and is then destroyed, erased, or anonymized.**

## 1. Scope

This policy covers personal information held in the [SERVICE NAME] application and its
backups. It does not cover information you provide to third parties directly, or data held
by the identity and email providers described in [section 9](#9-service-providers).

[SERVICE NAME] is a personal finance application. Everything it holds is entered by you or
generated by your use of the service; it does not purchase, enrich, or acquire personal
information from outside sources, and it does not sell personal information or use it for
advertising or automated decision-making.

## 2. What we hold, and why

| Category | What it includes | Why it is held |
|----------|------------------|----------------|
| **Financial records** | Transactions, amounts, dates, descriptions and notes, categories, accounts, budgets, recurring bills, investment contributions and withdrawals, and portfolio valuations | The service itself — the records you create and review |
| **Cost-sharing details** | Names of people you split bills with, their share, and whether they have paid | Shared-expense tracking you have chosen to use |
| **Account and profile** | Email address, display name, time zone, country, and preferences | Identifying you, signing you in, and formatting dates and currency |
| **Authentication data** | Password (stored only as a one-way hash), registered passkeys, recovery codes (hashed), single sign-on links, and API tokens (stored only as a hash) | Securing access to your account |
| **Security and activity records** | Sign-ins and failures, sign-outs, password and passkey changes, permission changes, exports, and account deletions — each with the time, the IP address, and the browser or client used | Detecting and investigating unauthorized access, and keeping privileged actions accountable |

**All of this is sensitive.** Transaction-level financial history can reveal health,
religion, political affiliation, and personal relationships. It is treated as sensitive
information in its entirety rather than graded by field.

## 3. How long we keep it

| Category | Retention period |
|----------|------------------|
| Financial records | **Until you delete them.** No automatic expiry — see [section 4](#4-why-financial-records-have-no-automatic-expiry) |
| Cost-sharing details | Until you delete the person, the bill they relate to, or your account |
| Account and profile | For the life of your account |
| Password, passkeys, recovery codes, sign-on links | For the life of your account, or until you remove the individual credential |
| API tokens | Until you revoke the token or delete your account |
| Security and activity records | **[365] days**, then deleted automatically |
| Password reset links | Unusable 30 minutes after they are issued |
| Sign-in security counters | Cleared automatically once the relevant time window closes |

Automated deletion runs continuously as part of normal operation rather than on a fixed
nightly schedule. On a lightly used deployment, removal of an expired record may therefore
lag the stated period by a short interval.

**We do not delete inactive accounts.** An account that goes unused is retained until you
ask us to close it. If you want your data removed, you must tell us — see
[section 7](#7-your-rights).

## 4. Why financial records have no automatic expiry

This is deliberate, and it is a retention decision made in your interest.

The purpose of a finance application is longitudinal: a five-year spending trend requires
five years of transactions, so "as long as necessary" for this purpose is "as long as you
want the history." You decide when a record stops being useful, not a timer.

Automatic deletion would also cut against your own record-keeping obligations. The Canada
Revenue Agency expects individuals to retain records supporting a tax return for **six
years** from the end of the relevant tax year, and this data can substantiate TFSA, RRSP,
and FHSA contribution room. Silently deleting a four-year-old transaction could destroy a
record you are required to hold.

**The consequence is that deletion is your action to take, so the tools to take it are
provided and documented in [section 7](#7-your-rights).**

## 5. How data is destroyed

**Closing your account** permanently destroys your financial records, cost-sharing details,
profile, password, passkeys, recovery codes, single sign-on links, and API tokens. It is
immediate and cannot be undone.

**Two exceptions are retained deliberately, and you should know about them before you ask
for erasure:**

1. **Security and activity records survive account closure.** They no longer link to your
   account, but a record that an account was deleted — including the email address it
   belonged to, and who deleted it — is kept so the deletion itself remains accountable.
   These records are removed at the end of the retention period in
   [section 3](#3-how-long-we-keep-it). This is permitted under GDPR Article 17(3), which
   preserves retention needed to establish or defend legal claims or to meet a legal
   obligation, and under PIPEDA's security safeguards principle. **It is time-limited, and
   that limit is what makes it lawful** — these records are not kept indefinitely.
2. **Configuration changes** record that a setting was changed, but the identity of the
   person who changed it is removed.

**Erasing your financial history without closing your account** is available separately: it
removes every transaction, account, budget, recurring bill, investment record, and
valuation, while keeping your login and security history intact.

**Destruction is complete, not merely a hidden flag.** Deleted records are removed from the
live database rather than marked inactive and retained. The storage they occupied is
reclaimed as part of routine maintenance, so that residual data does not persist on the
underlying medium.

## 6. Backups and snapshots

The service is backed up so that it can be restored after failure. **Backups are the real
limit on how quickly deletion becomes irreversible.**

- Backups and storage snapshots are retained for **[N] days**.
- A record you delete is removed from the live service immediately, but remains present in
  any backup taken before the deletion until that backup expires.
- **Your erasure request is fully effective after [N] days**, once every backup predating it
  has rotated out. Restoring a backup within that window would reinstate deleted data; if
  that happens, deletions are re-applied.
- Backups are encrypted at rest and access is limited to administrators.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of the following rights. We respond within
**30 days** (PIPEDA and Law 25) or **one month** (GDPR), and there is no charge.

| Right | What you get | How to exercise it |
|-------|--------------|--------------------|
| **Access** | A complete copy of the personal information held about you | Export your data in-app, under Settings → Data |
| **Portability** | The same copy in a structured, machine-readable format | As above — the export is JSON |
| **Correction** | Inaccurate information amended | Edit your records and profile directly in the app |
| **Erasure** | Your data destroyed, subject to [section 5](#5-how-data-is-destroyed) | Close your account, or erase your financial history only, under Settings |
| **Withdraw consent** | Processing stopped | Contact us — we will suspend and then close your account on request |
| **Restrict or object** | Processing halted without destroying data | Contact us to have your account suspended |

**Verification.** Before acting on a request that discloses or destroys data, we verify that
you are the account holder — normally by requiring you to be signed in and to confirm with
your second factor. An erasure request honoured for an impersonator would itself be a
privacy breach, so we do not waive this step.

**Two limits on the in-app export**, stated so you know what a complete request includes:

- Registered passkeys are excluded from the export file by design, because a passkey is
  bound to this service and restoring one into another account would be a means of account
  takeover. You can see each passkey's name, device, and enrolment date in the app.
- Your security and activity records are not part of the automated export. They are personal
  information about you and are within scope of an access request — **contact us and we will
  provide them.**

> ### Your exported file is sensitive
>
> The export contains your complete financial history together with credential material,
> including your password hash and any active single sign-on tokens. Treat the file as you
> would a password: store it encrypted, keep it off shared or cloud-synced drives, and
> delete it when you no longer need it. If an export is exposed, tell us — we will help you
> reset your credentials.

## 8. Data you provide about other people

If you record someone else for cost-sharing — a roommate, a partner — their name and payment
status is personal information about **them**, entered by you. They have no account and
cannot make a request through the application.

- Record as little as you need: a first name rather than a full legal name.
- If that person asks for their information to be removed, we will act on it. Contact us and
  we will remove them from the relevant records.

## 10. Security

Safeguards protecting retained information include: encryption of data in transit and of
backups at rest; passwords stored only as salted one-way hashes, never in recoverable form;
optional passkey-based multi-factor authentication, which once enabled is required at every
sign-in; rate limiting on authentication; scoped, expiring, revocable API tokens stored only
as hashes; strict separation so that each user's financial data is reachable only by that
user; and logging of privileged and destructive actions. Credentials and secrets are
excluded from log records before they are written.

## 11. Breach notification

If a breach of security safeguards creates a **real risk of significant harm** — the standard
under PIPEDA — we will:

- notify the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, and affected individuals, as soon
  as feasible;
- notify the relevant supervisory authority within **72 hours** where the GDPR applies, and
  affected individuals without undue delay where the risk is high;
- notify the *Commission d'accès à l'information* and affected individuals where Law 25
  applies; and
- record the incident in our breach register, which is **retained for 24 months** as PIPEDA
  requires, whether or not the breach was reportable.

Given the sensitivity of financial records, we treat exposure of transaction data,
credentials, or an export file as meeting the threshold for notification.
